# Stremio+ (personal mod build)

register: product

## Users
One person using Stremio on a Mac (desktop shell, dim room, big screen) to decide what to watch and then stream it. They browse, compare scores, glance at a trailer, then hit play.

## Product purpose
A Spicetify-style mod of stremio-web: adds Rotten Tomatoes scores, consensus, trailers and photos, plus quality-of-life UI mods, without changing how Stremio feels or works (addons, torrent streaming, library all untouched).

## Tone
"Stremio+": it should look like Stremio shipped these features itself. Calm, native, restrained.

## Anti-references
- Netflix imitation (black #181818 cards, white circle buttons, hover pop-out cards as the primary pattern).
- AI-slop UI: decorative gradients, tinted boxes with coloured borders, strokes/outlines around everything, glow shadows, gold gradient badges, uppercase letter-spaced eyebrow labels, card grids for everything.

## Principles
1. Reuse Stremio's own vocabulary before inventing anything (see DESIGN.md).
2. Content over chrome: scores and text sit on the surface; containers only where Stremio itself uses them.
3. Blur, not gradients, for legibility over artwork.
4. Accent colour only for primary actions and selection.
